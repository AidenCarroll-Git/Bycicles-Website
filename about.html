About Us...

Murray Bicycles & More started in 1998 in a one-bay garage behind founder Tom Murray's house. Tom was a weekend racer who got tired of driving two hours to find a shop that could true a wheel properly, so he started fixing neighbors' bikes out of his own workshop. Word spread quickly, and within two years he had a waiting list and a hand-painted sign out front.



In 2004, the shop moved into its current storefront on Main Street and added new bike sales, custom builds, and professional fittings. Tom's daughter Claire joined the team in 2012 and launched our online store, bringing the same careful service to riders far beyond town. Today, a team of twelve mechanics and sales staff carries on that original idea: treat every bike like it's your own, and every rider like a neighbor.



We still hand-assemble every bicycle we sell, and we still offer free adjustments for the life of your bike. Whether you're buying your first commuter, upgrading to a carbon road frame, or just need a flat fixed before work, we're glad you stopped by.
