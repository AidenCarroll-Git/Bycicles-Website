At Murray Bicycles & More, we've helped riders of every level find the right bike for over twenty years. From sleek road machines to dependable daily commuters, every bicycle we sell is hand-assembled, carefully tuned, and ready to ride the day you pick it up.


Our friendly team also offers expert repairs, custom bike fittings, and a full line of accessories and gear. Stop by during business hours or browse our selection online. Whether you're training for your first race or just riding for fun, we've got you covered.
